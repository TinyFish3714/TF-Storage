## 📖 Introduction / 简介

**TF Storage (TF存储)** 是一款专注于**大容量便捷存储**解决方案的 Minecraft 模组。

在原版游戏中，物品管理的混乱往往成为玩家最大的痛点。TF Storage 剥离了原本庞杂的功能，专注于核心的存储体验，通过精简的代码重构，为您带来更轻量、更高效的物资管理方案。

**TF Storage** is a Minecraft mod dedicated to **high-capacity and convenient storage** solutions.

Addressing the chaos of vanilla inventory management, TF Storage strips away the bloat to focus entirely on the core storage experience. Born from a thorough refactor, it delivers a lightweight and efficient way to manage your massive collection of resources.

## ✨ Key Features / 核心特性

*   **📦 Massive Capacity (海量存储)**
    Break free from the limits of standard chests. Store thousands of items in compact, space-saving blocks designed for hoarders and technical players alike.
    突破普通箱子的限制，在紧凑的空间内存储成千上万的物品，专为收集党和技术玩家设计。

*   **🛠️ Upgraded Bag Crafting (背包合成升级)**
    The TF Bag now features a full **3x3 crafting grid**, upgraded from the original 2x2. Craft items directly inside your bag without needing to carry a crafting table around.
    TF 背包内置合成栏全面升级为 **3x3** 标准大小（原为 2x2）。您无需再随身携带工作台，直接在背包内即可完成合成操作。

*   **⚡ Simplified & Optimized (精简优化)**
    A thorough refactor of the original codebase ensures better performance and stability. We've kept the best parts and removed the clutter.
    经过彻底的代码重构与精简，剔除了冗余功能，确保存储系统运行更加流畅稳定。

*   **🚀 Convenient Access (便捷存取)**
    Access your inventory seamlessly. Whether you are in your base or exploring the world, your resources are always within reach.
    无缝存取您的物品。无论您身处基地还是探索世界，物资管理都触手可及。

## 🔗 Compatibility & Integration / 兼容与联动

TF Storage is designed to play nice with other mods:
TF Storage 旨在与其他模组完美协作：

1.  **JEI Recipe Transfer (JEI 配方转移)**
    Full support for Just Enough Items (JEI) recipe transfer. Move items directly into the **TF Bag's internal crafting grid** with a single click.
    完美支持 JEI 配方转移功能，可一键将物品直接填充进 **TF 背包的内置合成栏**，无需依赖外部工作台。

2.  **Baubles Integration (饰品栏联动)**
    When the [Baubles](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/baubles) mod is installed, TF Storage bags gain Baubles slot capabilities, freeing up your inventory space.
    当同时加载 **Baubles (饰品栏)** 模组时，TF 背包/袋子将自动获得饰品属性，可直接装备在饰品栏中，解放您的背包空间。

3.  **FTB Sidebar Button (FTB 侧边栏支持)**
    Seamlessly integrates with FTB Library's sidebar buttons for quick UI access, keeping your screen clean and functional.
    无缝集成 FTB Library 侧边栏按钮，提供快捷的界面访问入口，保持游戏界面整洁高效。

## 🛠️ Installation & Compiling / 安装与编译

### Requirements
*   Minecraft 1.12.2
*   Forge 14.23.x
*   Java 8

### How to Build
1.  Clone this repository.
2.  Switch to `1.12.2` branch.
3.  Run `gradlew build`.
4.  Find the jar file in `build/libs/`.
